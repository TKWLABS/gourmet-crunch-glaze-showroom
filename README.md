# Gourmet Crunch Glaze Showroom

Welcome to the official showroom for **Gourmet Crunch Glaze** — premium coating solutions for artisanal confections.

## 🍫 About

Gourmet Crunch Glaze specializes in high-quality coatings and glazes for fine chocolates, pastries, and gourmet treats. Our products combine premium ingredients with innovative textures to create memorable culinary experiences.

## 🌟 Featured Products

- **Classic Chocolate Crunch** - Rich, decadent chocolate coating with satisfying crunch texture
- **Golden Honey Glaze** - Sweet honey-infused coating with delicate caramel notes
- **Strawberry Crunch** - Fresh strawberry flavor with natural fruit pieces
- **Pistachio Elegance** - Premium pistachio coating with smooth, nutty finish
