Chicken meal concepts from the research kitchen. These are experiments—refined, but exploratory.

Golden Fried Chicken

Buttermilk-brined bird, hand-dredged in a proprietary spice blend, fried to a shattering crunch. Served with house pickles and a warm biscuit.

Balmoral Chicken

A Scottish classic: chicken breast stuffed with haggis and wrapped in bacon. Finished with a red wine and mushroom gravy—warm, rich, and deeply savoury.

Herb-Roasted Chicken

Free-range bird, dry-brined for two days, roasted whole with thyme, rosemary, and lemon. Served with its own jus and crisp root vegetables.

The idea

Why now?

After 15 years in professional kitchens, I noticed something: comfort food had lost its craft. Mass production had made it convenient but forgettable. There's an opportunity to bring rigour back—to prove that simple food, made with intention, can be both accessible and exciting.

Gourmet Crunch & Glaze explores that middle ground. Not fine dining. Not casual. Something else—food that respects both the ingredient and the person eating it.

Right now, we're testing concepts. Feedback from people like you will determine whether we move forward, pivot, or close the kitchen.
